Overview

Everything GO PROCLAIM needs to launch

The work is split into two phases, so the site and paid membership can launch and start bringing in revenue before the community is built.

The site starts from the homepage design Tony shared. It covers the experiences, how it works, the destinations, the sample curriculum, the free India sample and testimonials. Paying members log in to a portal of GO PROCLAIM products, each linking to Amazon, and join a community for churches, schools and co-ops running the curriculum.

Everything runs on established services: Cloudflare for hosting, Supabase for logins and data, and Stripe for payments. GO PROCLAIM owns every account, so there's no lock-in to me as the developer.

Scope

What gets built

Phase 1

Website, membership & portal

$3,000
  • Production websiteThe homepage design rebuilt as a fast, mobile-friendly site on GO PROCLAIM's domain. Sample and newsletter forms connected. Remaining placeholders filled with content GO PROCLAIM provides.
